Design decisions:
- Joint loss = wCE + lambda_f*L_fair + lambda_c*L_cal + lambda_a*L_adv with the lambda on the loss value. The adversary head applies a GRL with coefficient 1 (pure reversal) so that lambda_a = 0 gives exactly the A2 loss and no influence on the trunk; trunk gradient from the adversary is -lambda_a x the plain-head gradient. Terms with effective lambda 0 are not computed.
- Soft flags: sigmoid((logit - batch quantile) / (T x batch std)), T = 0.1, quantile at 1 - flag_rate (0.10), threshold and std detached. The std scaling makes T scale-free (weighted CE inflates logits). eo = |soft TPR gap| + |soft FPR gap| (sum, slightly stricter than the reported max-based EOD); dp = |soft flag-rate gap|. A missing group or cell contributes 0 with a warning.

## Post-hoc tuning round PLAN: REF-T, ENS, REF-T-EG, ENS-EG (declared and committed BEFORE any run; post-hoc)
Owner request, declared after ALL test results are known (original, REF-EG, repeated splits). Added after the original test run, post-hoc. `configs/selection_rule.yaml`, existing results, the served model and the bundle stay unchanged. About 2 hours of compute, one process, at most 4 threads.
- **REF-T:** LightGBM, Optuna 40 trials, TPE sampler seed 42, 3-fold CV stratified on TARGET x gender (fold seed 42) on the split-42 TRAIN set only; objective mean fold AUC-ROC; early stopping inside each fold on that fold's held-out rows (200 rounds, up to 5000 trees, AUC only). Search: num_leaves 15 to 255 (log), learning_rate 0.01 to 0.1 (log), min_child_samples 10 to 300 (log), feature_fraction 0.3 to 1.0, bagging_fraction 0.5 to 1.0 (bagging_freq 1), lambda_l1 1e-3 to 10 (log), lambda_l2 1e-3 to 10 (log), max_bin 63 to 511. No class weights, as in REF. Time-box: Optuna timeout 70 minutes; if it stops early, the completed trial count is reported as a deviation from 40. Final model: best parameters, seeds 0, 1, 2, early stopping on validation AUC as in REF.
- **ENS:** rank-average of REF-T and S1 (the saved A2 models with prior correction), same seed paired. A score's rank is its empirical CDF on that seed's VALIDATION scores, so a test row's rank depends on no other test row. Score = w * rank(REF-T) + (1 - w) * rank(S1); w from {0, 0.1, ..., 1} chosen once by highest mean validation AUC-ROC over seeds. A rank average is not a probability, so ENS's probability is a Platt map of the score fitted on validation. It is monotone (AUC unchanged). ENS's validation ECE is therefore in-sample; only its test ECE is a fair reading. If w = 1, ENS equals REF-T in ranking, and this is reported.
- **REF-T-EG and ENS-EG:** built like REF-EG: `match_eg_weight` on train to a 10% flag rate, eps 0.01, scaler on train, score = pmf + 1e-6 * probability, gender in fit only, AUC and ECE from the arm's probability. z = REF-T TreeSHAP vector (bias dropped) for REF-T-EG; ENS-EG uses that vector concatenated with the A2 z. Gate: train flag rate within 0.01 of 0.10 on all 3 seeds, or no test access.
- Order: validation first with 1,000 paired bootstrap resamples: AUC-ROC, AUC-PR, worst-group ECE, EOD, DIR (and recall) vs REF, REF-EG, S2-EG. Then one Home Credit test access per passing arm, purposes `posthoc:REF-T`, `posthoc:ENS`, `posthoc:REF-T-EG`, `posthoc:ENS-EG`, predictions saved to `artifacts/test_preds/` at the access, metrics from saved files. Guard log 29 -> at most 33. No Taiwan, no repeated splits (their tuning data overlaps those test sets).
- Known weaknesses, disclosed now: REF-T gets 40 trials while the other arms got 10, which departs from CRITICAL_RULES 10 (equal tuning budget), so its gain must not be read as LightGBM vs TabTransformer on equal terms.
